Schools and education organisations produce a huge amount of material: strategic plans, prospectuses, reports, resources, books. LDA Creative turns that work into something clear, consistent and professional, designed by someone who understands education from the inside.

You've written the book. We make it ready for print and digital.

Whether it's a teaching resource, a professional text or a school history, we build it so it reads well and can be updated without starting over.
