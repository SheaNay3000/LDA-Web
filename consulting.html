LDA Consulting

Clear thinking for the decisions that shape your organisation

Every school and education organisation has big questions to answer: where are we heading, how will we get there, and how do we know it's working? LDA Consulting helps you answer them with plans and programmes grounded in how education actually works, because we've worked inside it.

Strategic planning

A plan your community understands and your staff can actually use.

We help senior leadership teams and boards build strategic and annual plans from the ground up, or sharpen the ones they already have. That means facilitating the conversations, gathering the voices that matter, and turning it all into a clear, well-structured document with goals that connect to day-to-day work. When it's ready to publish, LDA Creative can design it so it looks as considered as it reads.

Ideal for: boards and senior leadership teams entering a new planning cycle, schools whose current plan sits on a shelf.

Learning design

Programmes built with purpose, from the first idea to the finished resource.

We design curriculum, courses and learning programmes that are coherent, practical and aligned with what you're trying to achieve. That might be a new course, a redesigned programme, a staff learning pathway or a set of teaching resources. We map the outcomes, structure the learning and produce materials teachers can pick up and use.

Ideal for: schools developing new courses or pathways, organisations building training programmes, teams refreshing resources that have grown patchy over time.
